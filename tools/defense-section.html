Fifteen likely questions

  1. Calculate A* by hand on the chapter 2 map. Expand S. f(A) = 2 + 6 = 8, f(B) = 1 + 3 = 4. Expand B, then C with f = 3 + 1 = 4, then G with f = 5. Route S, B, C, G, cost 5.
  2. What is the difference between admissible and consistent? Admissible: never overestimates the remaining cost. Consistent: h(n) ≤ c(n, n′) + h(n′) on every edge. Consistent implies admissible.
  3. Calculate the minimax value and show which leaf alpha-beta prunes. min(3, 5) = 3, min(2, 9) = 2, max = 3, choose A. After A, α = 3; B's first leaf gives β = 2 ≤ α, so the 9 is skipped.
  4. Why does expectimax choose B? With 50/50 chance nodes, A = 4 and B = 5.5. Chance is averaged, not minimized.
  5. What is the acceptance probability in annealing for ΔE = 2 at T = 5? exp(−2/5) ≈ 0.670. At T = 0.5 it is about 0.018.
  6. What happens with a learning rate that is too large? On (w − 3)² with η = 1.1, every step overshoots further and w diverges. With η = 0.1, w goes 0.6, 1.08, 1.464.
  7. Compute the Bellman comparison. Shortcut 0.8 × 10 + 0.2 × (−5) = 7. Charge −1 + 0.9 × 9 = 7.1. Charge wins at γ = 0.9.
  8. Do one Q-learning update. Target 1 + 0.9 × 4 = 4.6. New Q = 2 + 0.5 × (4.6 − 2) = 3.3.
  9. What are α, γ and ε? α is the learning rate, γ the discount, ε the exploration rate in ε-greedy.
  10. Calculate the alarm posterior. 0.02 × 0.9 = 0.018; 0.98 × 0.1 = 0.098; 0.018 / 0.116 ≈ 15.5%.
  11. Filter two High readings in the HMM. First: [0.16, 0.14] normalized to [0.533, 0.467]. Predict Healthy 0.573. Weights [0.115, 0.299], so P(Faulty) ≈ 72.3%.
  12. Why does Viterbi give F, F? Joint scores: H,H 0.0288, H,F 0.0112, F,H 0.0056, F,F 0.0784. F,F is largest.
  13. Calculate precision, recall and F1 for TP 8, FP 12, FN 2. Precision 8/20 = 40%, recall 8/10 = 80%, F1 = 2 × 0.4 × 0.8 / 1.2 ≈ 53.3%.
  14. Why can one perceptron not learn XOR? XOR is not linearly separable. A hidden layer computing OR and AND makes it separable.
  15. How was generative AI used? Codex helped with the first edition; Claude Code helped with this edition's text, code, design and checks. Say what you reviewed yourself, and only what you actually reviewed.
